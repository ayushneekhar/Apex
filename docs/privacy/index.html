Apex Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Apex is a workout tracker for Android and iOS, developed by Ayush Neekhar ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

  • Apex has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your workout data is stored on your device. I never receive it.
  • If you turn on Google Drive backup, backups go straight from your device to your own Google Drive.
  • I don't sell, share or rent your data to anyone.

Data stored on your device

Apex stores the information you enter in a database on your device. This includes:

  • Workout templates, exercises and supersets
  • Logged sessions: sets, reps, weights, warm-up sets, rest times and dates
  • Notes you write on exercises or sessions
  • App settings, such as weight units and theme

This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to any server run by me. Because you may log information about your physical training, it is treated as personal fitness information. It is used only to show you your history, progress and statistics inside the app.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Apex asks for permission to create and manage only the files Apex creates in your Drive (the drive.file scope). Apex can't see or open any of your other Drive files.

  • Backups are uploaded directly from your device to your Google Drive. They never pass through a server run by me.
  • The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore-backed storage on Android). They are not sent to me.
  • When you tap "Disconnect Google Drive", Apex deletes these tokens from your device and asks Google to revoke its access.
  • Backups you've already made remain in your Drive until you delete them.

Apex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to back up and restore your Apex data. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to others, and not read by humans.

Google's handling of your Google account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Apex can show a notification when a rest timer ends. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification server is used, and no notification token is sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

App updates

To deliver bug fixes and improvements without a full store update, Apex checks for new versions of its code, which are hosted on GitHub (github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com). As with any web request, GitHub receives standard technical information such as your IP address. Apex does not send any of your workout data or any identifier with these requests. GitHub's handling of this information is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup system (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Android's backup to your Google account) may include Apex's data, depending on your device settings. These backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account and are not accessible to me.

What Apex doesn't do

  • No user accounts or sign-up
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health-platform access
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling Apex deletes all of its data from your device.
  • Google Drive backups: delete the Apex backup files from your Google Drive. To remove Apex's access, disconnect it in the app or visit your Google Account permissions.

Since I don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

Children

Apex is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as Apex starting to collect new types of data, I'll also tell you in the app.
